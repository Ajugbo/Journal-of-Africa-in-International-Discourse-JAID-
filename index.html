About the journal

JAID is a peer-reviewed, Diamond Open Access journal publishing rigorous scholarship on Africa's place in international debates. It examines how African states, societies and institutions shape, and are shaped by, global politics, economics, law, development, security, culture and ideas.

The journal welcomes work from political science, international relations, economics, history, law, sociology, anthropology, media and cultural studies, and related fields, including comparative and cross-regional studies that engage African scholarship directly. It accepts original research articles, critical essays, policy-relevant analyses and book reviews.

The journal charges no fees to authors or readers. All articles are published under CC BY 4.0 and reviewed by double-blind peer review.
